## 2. Reviewer Sentiment on Pricing (from `feedback.md`)

* **"Subscription Fatigue" (Top Theme):** Users despise paying recurring annual fees for what amounts to an offline logbook database.
* **"Data Hostage":** Users resent having export features paywalled after they've spent years entering receipts.
* **The "One-Time Unlock" Desire:** Automotive enthusiasts and DIYers repeatedly voice a willingness to pay **$10–$25 once** to own the software forever without recurring charges.

## 3. Noin Transparent Tier Model

We adopt a **Generous Free + Transparent Lifetime Unlock** strategy:

### Tier 1: Single Garage (Free Forever — $0.00)
* **Target:** Daily commuter, 1-car owner, DIY enthusiast.
* **Features:**
  * 1 Active Vehicle.
  * Custom Vehicle Photography.
  * Unlimited Service & Maintenance Logs.
  * Odometer & Status Tracking.
  * Mileage & Calendar Reminders.
  * Full CSV Data Export (Never held hostage).
  * 100% Offline & Ad-Free.

### Tier 2: Multi-Bay Garage (Lifetime License — $19.99 One-Time) 🏆 RECOMMENDED ANCHOR
* **Target:** Multi-car families, collectors, project car builders, fleet owners.
* **Features:**
  * Unlimited Garage Bays & Custom Car Photos.
  * Institutional-Grade Buyer Resale PDF Dossier Generator.
  * Unlimited Receipt & Documentation Photos.
  * Full CSV & JSON Data Export.
  * On-Device SQLite Backup & Restore.
  * Pay once, own forever. An honest lifetime plan.

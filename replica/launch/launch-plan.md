|---|---|---|---|
| **r/Cartalk** | Reddit (1.6M+ members) | DIY mechanics, daily commuters | High frustration over complex apps; people asking for maintenance spreadsheet templates. |

## 2. Organic Community Launch Strategy

### Step 1: The "Show Reddit / Built by Request" Post
* **Target:** `r/androidapps` & `r/Cartalk`
* **Headline:** *"I got tired of $40/year subscriptions just to track oil changes, so I built an offline, ad-free vehicle logbook that exports clean PDF records for buyers."*
* **Key Talking Points:**
  1. 100% offline — all records live on your phone in a local SQLite database.
  2. Free for 1 car with zero ads or tracking permissions.
  3. Supports classic cars (no 17-digit VIN check required).
  4. Generates a printable PDF Resale Dossier to maximize vehicle value when selling.
  5. Lifetime license option ($19.99) for those managing multi-car garages.

### Step 2: The Hacker News "Show HN"
* **Headline:** *"Show HN: TorqueKeep – A local-first, zero-telemetry vehicle logbook for Android"*
* **Angle:** Focus on local-first SQLite architecture, zero-cost operating model, and native on-device PDF synthesis without cloud APIs.

### Step 3: Product Hunt Launch
* **Tagline:** *"The offline vehicle maintenance logbook that buyers believe."*
* **Focus:** Speed of logging, tactile OLED dark UI, and anti-subscription pricing.

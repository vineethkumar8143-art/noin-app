## 2. The Problem (The Frustration with Current Apps)
* **The Subscription Trap:** Why pay $40 a year to track oil changes on a car you already bought?
* **Hostage Data:** Logging your car's service history for five years only for an app to get bought out, crash, or lock your records behind a paywall.
* **Intrusive Ads & Cloud Spying:** Cluttered banner ads right over entry buttons and invasive telemetry trackers monitoring your vehicle.

---

## 3. How Noin Works
1. **Park Your Vehicles & Add Custom Photos:** Add any car, truck, classic pre-1981 project, or motorcycle in seconds. Upload custom vehicle photos for each bay. No forced 17-digit VIN restrictions.
2. **One-Tap Maintenance Logging:** Tap dedicated service tiles for oil changes, brakes, tires, and batteries. Record mileage, parts used, and costs in under 10 seconds.
3. **Export Proof-of-Care Dossiers:** When selling your car or visiting a mechanic, generate a clean, chronological PDF report at the push of a button to prove maintenance and maximize resale value.

## 4. Key Differentiators (Built from Real Feedback)
* **100% Offline by Design:** Your records stay in a local database on your device. Zero internet connection required.
* **Unlocked CSV & PDF Exports:** Never get trapped. Export your complete raw dataset or print a physical glovebox folder whenever you want.
* **Zero Ads, Zero Telemetry:** No banner ads, no video pop-ups, no data harvesting.
* **Built for Car Owners & DIYers:** Large 48dp touch targets designed for gloved or oily hands in the garage bay.
